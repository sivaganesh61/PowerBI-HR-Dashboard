# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 🚀 Project Overview

The Workforce Analytics Dashboard is an end-to-end HR analytics solution developed in Power BI to provide comprehensive insights into employee demographics, compensation, performance, and organizational growth. The dashboard transforms raw HR data into interactive visual reports that help management and HR teams make informed workforce decisions.

The project analyzes key workforce metrics across multiple departments and countries, enabling stakeholders to understand employee distribution, salary structures, performance trends, and company growth patterns through an intuitive and interactive dashboard.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* 👥 Analyze workforce distribution across departments.
* ⚧️ Evaluate gender diversity within the organization.
* 🎂 Understand employee age demographics.
* 💰 Compare salary structures across departments.
* 🏆 Identify top-performing and highest-paid employees.
* 📈 Measure employee performance distribution.
* 📊 Track company growth trends over time.
* 🔍 Enable dynamic employee search and filtering.
* 🔗 Investigate the relationship between employee performance and salary.
* 🌏 Compare workforce metrics between India and New Zealand.

## 📋 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. 👥 Department-wise Employee Distribution

Analyzed the total number of employees working in each department to understand workforce allocation and departmental staffing levels.

### 2. ⚧️ Gender Distribution by Department

Examined the gender composition within each department to assess workforce diversity and representation across the organization.

### 3. 🎂 Employee Age Analysis

Studied the age distribution of employees to identify workforce demographics and age-group trends.

### 4. 💰 Salary Analysis by Department

Evaluated minimum, maximum, and average salary figures across departments to understand compensation structures and departmental pay variations.

### 5. 🏆 Top Earners by Country

Identified the highest-paid employees in each country, highlighting key contributors and compensation benchmarks.

### 6. 📈 Performance Distribution Analysis

Analyzed employee performance ratings to understand the distribution of high, medium, and low performers within the organization.

### 7. 📊 Company Growth Trend

Tracked employee hiring trends over time to visualize workforce expansion and organizational growth patterns.

### 8. 🔍 Employee Search & Filter

Implemented an interactive employee filter based on employee name initials, allowing users to quickly locate and analyze specific employee records.

### 9. 🔗 Performance vs Salary Analysis

Investigated the relationship between employee performance ratings and salary levels to determine whether compensation aligns with performance outcomes.

### 10. 🌏 India vs New Zealand Scorecard

Created a comparative scorecard to evaluate key workforce metrics between India and New Zealand, enabling country-level performance and workforce comparisons.
